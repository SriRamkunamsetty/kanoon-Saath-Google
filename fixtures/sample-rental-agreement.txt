RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
(Sample document for demonstration purposes only — not a real agreement)

This agreement is made between the Landlord and the Tenant for the residential
property located at Flat 4B, Sunrise Apartments, HSR Layout, Bengaluru.

1. Term. This lease begins on the 1st of the month and continues for 11 months,
   renewable by mutual written consent.

2. Rent. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000, payable in advance
   on or before the 5th day of each month.

3. Security Deposit. The Tenant shall pay a refundable security deposit equal
   to ten months' rent prior to occupying the premises.

4. Maintenance. The Tenant shall bear all maintenance charges levied by the
   apartment association, currently Rs. 2,500 per month, in addition to rent.

5. Termination. The Landlord may terminate this agreement at any time and
   without prior notice if the Landlord determines, in the Landlord's sole
   discretion, that the premises are required for personal use.

6. Lock-in Period. The Tenant may not vacate the premises within the first
   6 months of the term under any circumstances. Any request to vacate early
   will result in forfeiture of the entire security deposit.

7. Repairs. The Tenant is responsible for all repairs to fixtures and fittings
   during the tenancy, including repairs arising from normal wear and tear.

8. Notice to Vacate. The Tenant must provide 60 days' written notice before
   vacating at the end of the term.

9. Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising from this agreement shall be
   resolved exclusively through arbitration in a city chosen by the Landlord,
   with the Tenant bearing all arbitration costs.

10. Governing Law. This agreement is governed by the laws applicable in the
    state where the property is located.
